# Biological/Digital AI Convergence & Organism-like Architectures (2026)

AI systems are increasingly mirroring biological complexity, moving from simple reactive models to stateful, autonomous organisms capable of directed evolution and complex life-cycles (Perception-Reasoning-Action-Reflection or PRAR).

## Biological AI: PROTEUS
- **Concept:** PROTein Evolution Using Selection (PROTEUS) uses "biological artificial intelligence" to conduct directed evolution within mammalian cells. 
- **Mechanism:** Performs rapid trial-and-error testing inside living cells instead of a digital substrate, functioning as a biological machine for discovering medicines and gene-editing tools.

## Organism-Like AI Agents
- **Generative Biology (Evo 2):** Foundation models operating at the genome scale to "read" and "write" DNA. They employ architectures (like StripedHyena) to scale memory and compute for biological simulations.
- **Agentic Frameworks (e.g., LangGraph):** Serve as the "nervous system" for digital agents, allowing them to manage state, loop, and recover from failures, similar to biological resilience. Agents are treated as evolutionary individuals in cognitive-platform designs.
- **Bio-Digital Integration:** The convergence extends to Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs) and neuroadaptive systems, adjusting digital responses to real-time human cognitive and biological states.
